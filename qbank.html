    🧭 How the ratings work

    • Chance %: estimated probability that this question or a close variant appears in your paper. Asked in 2–3 past papers → 75–95%; asked once → 55–80%; core syllabus but never asked ("due") → 30–60%; minor → below 35%.
    • Priority: 🔥 Must (≥ 70%) · ⭐ High (50–69%) · ✔ Medium (30–49%) · Low (< 30%).
    • Weightage: the marks forms it can come in: 2 (Part A), 4 (Part B), 10 (Part C). Answers are written for the biggest form; the blue box is the 2-mark version.
    • Difficulty: ★ (very easy) to ★★★★★ (tough).
    • Based on only 3 RTU papers (2024 Part A, 2025 and 7CAI 2025), so treat the % as guidance, not a guarantee.
